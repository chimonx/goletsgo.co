Bus luggage rules and what to bring

How much luggage you can take on a Thai intercity bus, what to keep with you, and a packing checklist for overnight coach travel.

Thai intercity buses are generous with luggage compared to airlines, but a little planning keeps your trip smooth, especially overnight.

How much can you bring?

Most operators allow one or two large bags in the hold underneath plus a small bag you keep with you. There is rarely a strict weight limit, but oversized or very heavy items may be refused or charged. If you are carrying a lot, a private drive avoids the hassle entirely.

Keep with you

  • Passport, wallet and phone — never in the hold.
  • A warm layer for the air-conditioning.
  • Water, snacks and any medication.
  • A power bank; not every bus has working sockets.

In the hold

  • Large backpacks and suitcases.
  • Anything bulky or awkward to hold for hours.
  • Take a photo of your bag tag if the crew gives you one.

Travelling with a group and lots of bags? Compare the total cost against a private drive — it is often close.
